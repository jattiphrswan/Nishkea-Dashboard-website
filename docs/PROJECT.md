# Project brief
## Goal
One source of truth for product designs, variant stock, sales, purchasing, customers and daily team work, with a premium public shopping experience.

## Starting assortment
- Earrings: 20+ designs, normally sold by pair.
- Rings: 30+ designs, sizes may vary.
- Hair bands/scrunchies: 30+ designs.
- Necklaces: approximately 39 designs.
- Nose rings: 40+ designs.
At least approximately 159 designs before colour/size variants; quantities are planning estimates.

## Audiences
Shoppers, repeat customers, owner, manager, sales/support, inventory staff and accounts staff.

## Key outcomes
Accurate available stock; no overselling; clear monthly collection decisions; faster packing and follow-ups; visibility into unsold stock cost; consistent premium brand presentation.

## Assumptions requiring confirmation
Fashion jewellery/accessories and purchased finished goods; INR is a provisional display currency; India is a provisional operating market. Manufacturing, precious-metal weight pricing, hallmark workflows and country-specific taxes are not assumed. Capture those requirements separately if relevant.

## Scope boundaries
Public website and private dashboard share business services. Basic payment and expense records are included; full statutory accounting/payroll is deferred to an accounting integration. Marketing message delivery and marketplace integrations are later work. Policy text requires business-supplied rules before launch.
