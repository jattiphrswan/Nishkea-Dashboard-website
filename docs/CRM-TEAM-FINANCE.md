# CRM, daily work and finance
## CRM
Customer profiles, retail/wholesale segment, enquiries, owner, lead source, stages enquiry → qualified → quotation → won/lost, next follow-up, notes and order history. Lost reasons and duplicate-contact review. Marketing consent stored separately. No automatic WhatsApp/email sending before provider and consent workflows exist.

## Team
Owner/manager assigns tasks with due dates, priority, linked product/order/review and status todo/in progress/blocked/done. Daily updates and manager views. Recurring tasks generate once per scheduled occurrence. Attendance/payroll deferred unless confirmed.

## Finance
Customer receipts, outstanding invoices, supplier payments, purchase dues, approved operating expenses, refunds, daily reconciliation and CSV export. Record partial payments and allocations; never infer payment merely from order status. Gross margin = net merchandise revenue excluding collected tax − allocated cost of goods sold; display shipping/payment/operating expenses separately. Net profit requires a complete expense/accounting model and is not claimed by initial reports.

## Content operations
Staff manage approved homepage sections, collections, FAQs, care information and chatbot knowledge. Publication is role-restricted; preview drafts before publishing. No fake reviews. Support tickets track owner, priority, customer/order reference and resolution.
