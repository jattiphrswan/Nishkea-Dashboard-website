# Content and asset checklist
## Needed from business
Original logo SVG/transparent PNG, exact brand name/tagline, official palette, licensed fonts if any, domain/contact details, product sheets with SKUs/options/prices/opening stock, supplier list, approved product photography, shipping/return/payment policies and customer-support process.

## Photography
Consistent neutral background; front/detail/scale views; jewellery worn images only with appropriate rights; colour-consistent variants. Record source/licence. Do not extract commercial product images from the supplied search screenshot. Use labelled placeholders until approved assets arrive.

## Import sheets
Products: design_code,name,category,collection_type,launch_date,review_date,lifecycle.
Variants: design_code,sku,colour,size,sales_unit,price_minor,currency.
Opening stock: sku,location,batch_reference,received_date,sellable_qty,damaged_qty,unit_cost_minor.
Validate identifiers, units, duplicates and totals in a dry-run preview; import only after review. Keep fixtures clearly fictional and separate from actual imports.

## Missing decisions
Exact logo colours; retail/wholesale scope; countries/currency; operating timezone; shipping zones; taxes; payment options; return restrictions; staff count; warehouses; domain and hosting; AI provider/budget. These do not block a local visual scaffold, but must be resolved before production-dependent nodes.
